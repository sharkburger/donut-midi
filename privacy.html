Donut MIDI Neon — Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for the Mac App Store edition. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Contact: bx2008@nyu.edu.

What the app processes

Mouse performance uses pointer input and a simulated pupil slider. Live Neon mode receives gaze, pupil measurements when available, tracking quality, eye geometry and matched scene frames from your compatible Neon Companion device over the local network. Scene frames are used to locate the four AprilTag corners. The connector retains the latest sample for the instrument; it does not save eye-camera or scene video.

The app does not infer emotions or provide a medical or psychological diagnosis. Sound mappings and self-reports are creative or researcher-selected inputs.

Local data and recordings

Optional participant tests and recordings use explicit consent and start controls. Participant IDs, session samples, note or task events, and self-reports can be stored in a local SQLite database inside the app's macOS sandbox. Ordinary in-memory recordings must be exported to be retained after the session. Workshop drafts may be retained in the current web session; export a file to keep a separate copy.

The app does not automatically migrate an older desktop edition's database. Files you explicitly import or export remain under your control.

Data leaving the Mac

The application is designed without advertising, analytics, a developer-operated cloud account, or automatic upload of participant sessions to the developer. Communication with Neon Companion occurs on your local network. When you export or share a file, send a support email, or follow an external web link, the destination you choose handles that information under its own policies. Do not include identifiable participant data in support requests unless you intend to disclose it.

Apple handles App Store purchases, distribution and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's policies. Pupil Labs' device and Companion software have their own privacy practices, separate from this app.

Retention and deletion

Exported files remain until you delete them from the destination you selected. Local participant-session data persists in the app container; closing a browser view does not delete the database. The current edition does not yet offer a verified in-app per-session deletion workflow. Contact support for guidance before removing local application data, as deleting it can permanently remove unexported sessions.

Permissions and security

The app requests local-network access for Neon and access to files selected through macOS open/save dialogs. The instrument does not require microphone recording or the Mac's camera. Local connector APIs use a random per-launch pairing credential and bind to the Mac's loopback interface. User sketch previews use a restricted browser sandbox and do not receive the pairing credential.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change.
