Donut MIDI Neon — Support

Donut MIDI Neon is an experimental eight-key musical instrument for Mac. The app is free, with no in-app purchases.

Contact

Email bx2008@nyu.edu. Include your Mac model, macOS version, app version, the steps that produced the issue, and the exact error message. Do not send Apple passwords, pairing codes, private signing keys, or participant recordings unless you deliberately choose to share the recording for troubleshooting.

Play without eye-tracking hardware

Choose Mouse simulation, click Enable sound & play, and hold the pointer over a donut. Move away and return to retrigger. You can assign notes, adjust dwell and volume, and replay JSON recordings. The pupil slider is simulated input; it does not measure eyes or identify emotions.

Live Neon input

Live gaze requires compatible Pupil Labs Neon glasses connected to Neon Companion. Keep the Mac and Companion phone on the same local network, keep Companion running, and allow Donut MIDI Neon to access the local network when prompted. Use Pair Neon → Connect in the app. Keep all four AprilTag corners visible to the glasses' scene camera. Missing or occluded tags invalidate the surface mapping.

MIDI

Use the Sound panel's MIDI section to choose a CoreMIDI output destination. Connect a MIDI device or configure an IAC destination and open an instrument in the receiving software. Local synthesized sounds use the Mac audio output; they do not require an external MIDI device.

Recordings, participant sessions and exports

Recording and participant-session collection require the app's consent and start controls. Export in-memory recordings before closing the app. Participant sessions are stored in the app's local sandbox database. Use the Research controls to export CSV packages. Save exported recordings, sketches, layouts and ZIPs using the macOS save dialog.

Workshop

Workshop opens in a separate app window. Choose a template, edit it, run its preview, and export your sketch or matching mat. Drafts in the current web session are not a substitute for saving a file. Sketch previews restrict external network requests.

Privacy

Read the accompanying Privacy Policy. The app is a creative and research tool, not a diagnostic device or a validated cognitive-state classifier.
